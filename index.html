Law, Environment and Development Centre

The Law, Environment and Development Centre (LEDC), based in the College of Law at SOAS University of London, investigates the dynamic relationships between law, environment and development. Its research focuses in particular on topics of relevance to the Global South and to North–South relations, and its teaching combines core subjects in environmental law with a critical inquiry into the theory and practice of the field.
