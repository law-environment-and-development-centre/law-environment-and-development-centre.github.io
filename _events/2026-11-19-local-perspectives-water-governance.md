LEDC Seminar Series. Three SOAS PhD candidates explore how water is understood, accessed and governed in different local settings, with attention to gender, property rights, customary law and community perspectives.

- Labiba Rukhsana: Gender, Health and Water in Coastal Bangladesh
- Harendra Beniwal: Whose Water? Property Rights and Everyday Water Access in Rajasthan, India
- Marta Simonetti: Subak in Bali: Customary Law and the Ontological Perspective of Water Governance

All are welcome.
