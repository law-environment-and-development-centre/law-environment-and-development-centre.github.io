LEDC Seminar Series 2026, with Dr Matthew Gillett (Senior Lecturer, University of Essex, and Vice-Chair Rapporteur of the UN Working Group on Arbitrary Detention). Ecocide is increasingly being adopted at the domestic level and has been proposed for inclusion in the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court. The seminar looks at the question of legality in distinguishing criminal conduct from beneficial contributions to communal life.
