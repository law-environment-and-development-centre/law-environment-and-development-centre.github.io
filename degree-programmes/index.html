LLM/MA modules

The SOAS LLM/MA in Environmental Law and Sustainable Development offers a wide range of distinctive modules that combine a focus on core subjects in the field with a critical inquiry into the theory and practice of environmental law, particularly as they relate to the global South. Each module page below gives more background than the SOAS course pages.

Semester 1

  • Law, Environment and Social Justice 15 credits
  • Law and Natural Resources: Framework, Rights and Land 15 credits
  • International Environmental Law 15 credits
  • Water Justice: Rights, Access and Movements 15 credits

Semester 2

  • Law and Natural Resources: Biological Resources, Corporations and Mining 15 credits
  • Law and the Climate Crisis 15 credits
  • Alternatives to Sustainable Development: Rights of Nature and Harmony with Nature 15 credits
  • Law, Environment, and the Global Commons: Ice, Sea, Space and Beyond 15 credits
